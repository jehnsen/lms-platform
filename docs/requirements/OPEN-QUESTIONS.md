# Open questions for the client

Points the BRD and the BA deliverables leave open, or where the two disagree. Nothing here has been decided on the client's behalf: each row records the **default the app uses for now**, and every default is either configurable or isolated in one place so it can change without rework.

**Last updated:** 2026-10-14, after Phase 5 and the Q-25 change. New in Phase 5: Q-56 to Q-62; defaults recorded for Q-04 and Q-19, and Q-34 extended to class times. Q-25 was decided on 2026-10-14 and built; what it left open is now Q-63. **Q-18 (the SIS export format) is needed before go-live.**

Status: `Open` (waiting for an answer), `Answered` (decision recorded in the row), `Deferred` (not needed until a later phase).

| Q-37 | What may students hand in: which file types, how large, how many? | FR-3.3 | PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT, MD, PPT, PPTX, ODP, JPG and PNG; 25 MB each (an admin setting); at most 5 files per hand-in. | Open |

| Q-44 | May a teacher change a mark that was given automatically, for example after a wrong answer key? | FR-3.6, US-12 | No. Only teacher-marked answers can be marked or re-marked. Once any student has taken a quiz its questions are locked, so a wrong key cannot be corrected. | Open |

| Q-17 | Grading scale bands and the GPA mapping; default grade categories and weights; and who sets each. | FR-4.1, FR-4.2, US-16 | Three scales: Percentage, Letter grades (A 93, A− 90, B+ 87, B 83, B− 80, C+ 77, C 73, C− 70, D 60, F 0) and GPA 4.0 (the same bands worth 4.0 down to 0.0). An admin changes the grades of the letter and GPA scales in Settings and picks the scale a course starts with (letter). The teacher picks the scale for each course and sets its categories and weights; a course nobody has set up uses Assignments 50% and Quizzes 50%. Admins cannot add a fourth scale yet. | Open |

| Q-48 | Inside a grade category, does each piece of work count equally, or in proportion to its points? | FR-4.1, FR-4.2 | In proportion to its points: a 50-point project counts five times a 10-point quiz in the same category. | Open |
| Q-49 | Does work that was never handed in count as zero in the course grade? If so, from when: the due date, or the end of term? | FR-4.2, US-17 | A per-course option the teacher sets, off by default: off leaves ungraded work out, so the total is "the grade so far"; on counts work as zero once its due date has passed. With it off, a student who handed in one good piece of work and nothing else has a high final grade, so the school should decide this before final grades are exported. | Open |
| Q-50 | Do courses carry credits or units, so that a GPA across courses weights them? | FR-4.2, FR-4.4 | No credits. The term GPA and the term average on a progress report count every course equally. | Open |
| Q-51 | Should quiz scores be shown as soon as a student hands in, and may a teacher hold them? | Q-52 | Who may export grades to the SIS, when, and what happens when a grade is exported a second time? | FR-4.5, US-18 | Administrators only (US-18 is the administrator's story), at any time, for one course or a whole term, as often as they like. What a repeat export does to grades already in the SIS is for the SIS to decide. | Open |
| Q-53 | Which grades must be held back from an SIS export? | FR-4.5, US-18 | A record fails, with the reason shown, when the account has no SIS ID, nothing is graded, some work is handed in but not marked, or some marks are not released to the student. Work that was never handed in does not block the export. | Open |
| Q-54 | How are final grades rounded, and is there a rule for borderline grades? | FR-4.2 | Totals are worked out exactly and rounded to a whole percent at the end, halves up; the grade on the scale is read from the rounded percent, so 89.5% is 90% and an A−. | Open |
| Q-55 | What must a progress report or report card contain beyond grades: attendance, teacher comments, conduct, a signature, the school's layout? | FR-4.4, US-18 | For each course: section, teachers, the grade by category, the total, the grade on the course's scale, work missing and lessons done; then the term average and GPA. Attendance joins it in Phase 5. No teacher comments, conduct or school branding. | Open |
| Q-19 | The attendance threshold; how Late and Excused count towards it; the window in which attendance can be edited. | FR-5.2, FR-5.4, US-19, US-21 | All admin settings. Threshold 80%. Late counts as attended (the alternatives are half a session, or missed). Excused is left out of the rate (the alternatives are attended, or missed). The threshold is not checked until 5 sessions count for the student in the course. A submitted session can be corrected for 48 hours after it was first submitted. | Open |
| Q-56 | Is the attendance threshold applied to each course, or to a student's attendance across all their courses, or to days at school? | FR-5.4, FR-5.5, US-21 | To each course: a student can be below it in one course and not in another, and the alert names the course. | Open |
| Q-57 | Once the correction window has closed, can anyone still correct an attendance record, for example the school office with a doctor's note? | FR-5.2, US-19 | No. The record is locked for everyone, admins included. The only way round it is for an admin to lengthen the window. | Open |
| Q-58 | Who may take attendance for a section: only its teachers, any teacher of the course, a substitute, the office? And how far back can a missed session be taken? | FR-5.1, US-19 | Only the teachers assigned to the section. A session with no record can be taken on its day or any day after it, with no limit; never ahead of its day. | Open |
| Q-59 | When the attendance policy changes, should students the new rule puts below the threshold be alerted straight away? | FR-5.4, FR-5.5 | Yes. Saving a new threshold or counting rule re-checks everyone at once; raising the threshold can send many alerts in one go. | Open |
| Q-60 | What is an "exam" on the calendar, and who keeps the academic calendar? | FR-5.3, US-20 | Exams, holidays and other events are calendar entries. An admin adds them for the whole school; faculty add them for the courses they teach (an exam, a cancelled class). Quizzes and assignments appear as due dates, not as exams. An entry marked "no classes" takes classes off the timetable for the school, or for its course. | Open |
| Q-61 | If a holiday or a cancelled class is added for a day on which attendance was already taken, what happens to that record? | FR-5.1, FR-5.3 | The record is kept and still counts towards the rate; the session just no longer appears in the list. | Open |
| Q-62 | Should people be told when the calendar changes (a new exam, a cancelled class, a moved deadline), and should the calendar be available as a feed for phone and desktop calendar apps? | FR-5.3, FR-6.6 | No notification and no feed. The calendar itself is always current. | Open |
| Q-63 | What else should an import or SIS sync do to an existing account: move a student to another section of the same course, take someone out of a section, update a changed name or email, change a role, or deactivate people who have left? | US-01, US-04, FR-1.6, FR-8.1 | None of these. A row that would move a student between sections of one course, or that gives a different role, is reported as an error and changes nothing. A deactivated or archived account is not placed in sections until an admin reactivates it. Names, emails and statuses are only changed on the Users page. | Open |
